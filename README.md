Website goals and objectives

Help Bright Beginnings reach more people in their community so they can receive more donations and attract more volunteers towards their cause Make it easier for Bright Beginnings to provide people with updates If people Google Bright Beginnings, they will find the website and be able to contact them and get a location of their offices if needed People are also able to see what Bright Beginnings is about, as they have an About Usa page on their website Provide Bright Beginnings and their donors with a safe and secure payment option -Expand community engagement References: Paxtree 2025, How much should you budget for your new app in South Africa - paxtree, Paxtree, viewed 28 August 2026, https://www.paxtree.co.za/new-app-budgeting-south-africa/. 🗂️ Project Structure & Pages: The project consists of five core HTML files designed to fulfill the site architecture requirements:

File Name Page Description Key Components home-page.html Homepage Navigation menu, Call-to-Action header buttons, centered hero section, medium-sized feature image, introduction. aboutus-page.html About Us: Organizational history, core mission, vision statement, and structured leadership team section. volunteer-page.html Volunteer Community engagement information and an integrated volunteer application form (with interest checkboxes and dropdowns). donate-page.html Donate: Direct banking details table (EFT details) and a secure online credit card donation form. contactus-page.html Contact Us: branch details (Durban HQ and Westville branch), dual interactive Google Maps, and a direct inquiry
